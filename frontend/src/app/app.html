Wildfire Building Damage Predictor

Enter the surroundings and weather around a building to estimate whether it would be damaged in a California wildfire. Model: Random Forest (comprehensive model, 21 inputs), based on Bar et al., Science Advances 2026.

Research reproduction for a DevOps lab. This is not an operational risk assessment.
